# Hi, I'm Adharsh 👋

**Supply chain & industrial engineering | MS Industrial Engineering, Purdue University | 3 years at Kinaxis**

I help supply chains plan better, using simulation, forecasting and optimization. I came from consulting on planning systems at Kinaxis, and I'm now pairing that practitioner view with the analytical methods behind it.

## What I work on

- **Supply chain planning:** capacity planning, S&OP, demand forecasting, MRP, inventory management
- **Simulation:** discrete-event models in Arena, fab-level simulation (SMT2020 datasets), MATLAB and Simulink
- **Forecasting and ML:** ARIMA, XGBoost, Random Forest, ANN, CNN, LSTM, k-NN, Kalman filters
- **Optimization and quality:** mixed-integer programming, genetic algorithms, SPC and DMAIC
- **Tools:** Python, SQL, R, MATLAB and Simulink, Arena
